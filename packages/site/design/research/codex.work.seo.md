## Research-aligned content pattern

Research shows prompt-card examples and practical tone. For SEO:
- Prompt examples should be **indexable text**, not only images.
- Each example card can target a long-tail task query.
- Avoid duplicating developer page jargon.

## Recommendations

1. Unique title: work outcomes, not “for developers” echo.
2. H1 + subhead from research (“Work faster with Codex”) — good human CTR.
3. Visible text transcripts of example prompts (accessibility + SEO).
4. Business trust blocks: admin, security, SSO — target “ChatGPT Business agents” queries.
5. Clear canonical; no parameter variants.
6. Internal links to Business pricing and security pages.
7. Validate SSR for LinkedIn/Slack unfurls (B2B sharing matters).
