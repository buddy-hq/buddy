## Hard Rule
use when user asks for luna subagents. not applicable for all delegations.


## Guidelines
- use luna on only 'max' reasoning.
- can use as many subagents in parallel [Hard Cap: 10 at a time]


## What can Luna do

- Luna can make predictable, mechanical edits.
- Luna can implement simple plans with clear outcomes, established solutions and predictable paths.

## What CAN'T Luna do
- Luna can't make decisions that require careful judgement.
- Luna doesn't consider second order effects.
- Luna can't do system design.
- Luna doesn't have engineering taste.
- Luna can't reliably make changes that affect multiple systems.

## What Luna needs
- clear start point
- clear final state
- clear path to follow

## Trust BUT Verify
- The delegator is responsible for changes that Luna makes.
- Delegator checks/reviews all luna code.
