# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent to people.

## What that means

Buddy is a project about learning, and people asking questions are the point of it. Assume the person on the other end knows things you don't, and is missing things you know. Answer the question that was asked.

Behaviour that will get you removed: harassment of any kind, insults aimed at a person rather than an argument, sexual attention or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

Disagreement is fine. Disagreement is most of the value of a public issue tracker. Strong opinions about code are welcome, opinions about a person's worth are not, and the line between those two is not subtle.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by messaging the maintainer, [@prashantbhudwal](https://github.com/prashantbhudwal), through GitHub. For behaviour that breaks GitHub's own rules, [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) reaches staff who can act across the whole platform, which is the better route when someone needs stopping beyond this one repository.

Expect a reply within a week. The response depends on what happened, and ranges from a private word to a permanent ban. If you disagree with a decision, say so in the same thread and it will get a second look.

The maintainer is subject to this document too. If the problem is the maintainer's behaviour, saying so publicly in an issue is a legitimate move.
